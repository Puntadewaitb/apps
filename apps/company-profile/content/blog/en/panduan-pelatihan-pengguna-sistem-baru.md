Many new systems are abandoned within the first three months because users feel **unprepared**. Good training can prevent that.

## Principles of effective training

- Use **real cases** from your company, not generic examples.
- Split sessions by role: cashiers, admins, and leaders need different things.
- Provide a one-page quick guide for each main task.

## Practical steps

1. Measure participants' starting skills with a short quiz.
2. Practice directly in a test environment, not with live data.
3. Appoint one "champion" in each unit as the go-to person for questions.
4. Evaluate after two weeks and fix whatever is still confusing.
