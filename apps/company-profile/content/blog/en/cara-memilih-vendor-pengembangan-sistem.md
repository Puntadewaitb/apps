Choosing a system development vendor is a long-term decision. Here is the checklist we recommend.

## Must-ask questions before the contract

1. Who owns the **source code** after the project ends?
2. Is there technical documentation and a user guide?
3. How are maintenance and bug fixes handled?
4. What technology is used — is it common and easy to hire for?
5. How is your data backed up and secured?

## Red flags

- The vendor refuses to hand over code or server access.
- There are no similar projects whose references you can contact.
- An estimate without a breakdown of the work.

## Start small

Ask for a **small but real** first release within 4–6 weeks. From it you can judge the quality of communication and delivery before committing to more.
