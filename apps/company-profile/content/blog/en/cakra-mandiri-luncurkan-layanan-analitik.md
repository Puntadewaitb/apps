Jakarta — Cakra Mandiri today announced its **Analytics & Data Dashboard** service for mid-sized companies.

## What is offered

The service unifies data from multiple sources, defines indicators together with leadership, then presents them in a dashboard that is easy to read on a phone.

> "Many leaders have data but no single number they can trust. That is what we solve." — Citra Maharani, Head of Data Analytics

The service can be ordered now. Schedule an initial consultation via the [contact page](/en/kontak) or read the [service details](/en/layanan/analitik-data).
