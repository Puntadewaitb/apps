Launching a new system is not the finish line. The real measure of success is **whether the work gets better**.

## Three simple indicators

### Time

How long does the process take before and after the new system? Record it before the project starts so you have something to compare against.

### Errors

Count the corrections or rework per week. A drop here is felt directly in costs.

### Usage

The best system is useless if it isn't used. Track what percentage of users are active each week.

## Set them up front

Agree on all three indicators **before** building starts, with targets and how to measure them. An example of a simple dashboard can be seen in the [multi-branch sales dashboard](/en/portofolio/dasbor-penjualan-lestari) project.
