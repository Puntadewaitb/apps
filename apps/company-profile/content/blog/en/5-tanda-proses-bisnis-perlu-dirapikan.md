Many companies only realize their processes are a mess once the problem is expensive. Here are five signs you can check **today**.

## 1. The same work is done twice

The same data is retyped across several files or systems. Besides wasting time, this is the main source of *mismatched numbers* between reports.

## 2. Everyone asks the same person

If knowledge lives in one person's head, your process is fragile. When that person is on leave, work stops.

## 3. Delays with no owner

Work gets stuck between two divisions and nobody feels responsible for it.

## 4. Performance is hard to measure

If a simple question like "how long does an order take on average?" takes a day to answer, your indicators don't exist yet.

## 5. Customers keep asking for status

The question "where is it now?" signals that your flow isn't transparent.

> Start with the one process that causes the most complaints, then map it step by step.
